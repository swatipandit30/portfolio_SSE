# Swati Pandit | Portfolio

Personal portfolio of **Swati Pandit**, Senior Software Engineer with 6 years of experience in cloud, integration and DevOps.

**Live site:** https://swatipandit30.github.io/portfolio_SSE/
**CV (PDF):** Inside Portfolio website - txt CV available, and Original CV available to Recruiters only

## What's on the site

- **About** with a short summary and a longer story you can open
- **My journey** through four career chapters, from college to technical leadership
- **Impact in numbers** with animated counters, a "what I cut" bar chart and a technology timeline
- **Selected work** with delivered projects and the assistants currently in development
- **How I lead** with three guiding habits and a deck of flip cards
- **Skills** you can search and filter by employer
- **Credentials** covering certifications, education and languages
- **Beyond the day job** covering training sessions, talks, hackathons and meetups

## Built with

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript in a single file (`index.html`). No build step, no frameworks and no dependencies, apart from two Google Fonts. It is responsive, supports light and dark themes, and respects the reduced-motion setting.

Download `index.html` and open it in any browser. Nothing else is needed.
